Systems Status

race priorities
Premix & seize risk
No oil injection. Mix race oil into every fill at the tuned ratio. A miss — or a crank-seal air leak — seizes it.
Top end (life-limited)
Pistons/rings are a scheduled wear item. Log race-hours; measure bore + ring gap; rebuild on cadence.
Jetting
Carb tune by air temp/density/altitude. Read plug chops every session — jet rich when unsure.
Crank & seals
Pressure-test the crankcase; a hardened seal draws air → lean → seizure.
Gearing
Sprockets set per circuit. Log ratios vs trap speed and corner exit.
Chassis / tires
Slick compound + heat cycles, tire warmers, suspension clicks logged per session.

05

Diagnosis

No ECU, no fault codes — the garage's Code Lookup doesn't apply. A race two-stroke is tuned by feel and evidence: plug chops for jetting, compression + crankcase pressure for engine health, data/trap speed for gearing, and a rebuild log by race-hours. The safe default is the same as the RZ — jet rich, read the plug. A lean TZ is a seized TZ, and this one has no oil injection to fall back on.
