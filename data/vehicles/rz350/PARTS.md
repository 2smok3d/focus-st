# RZ350 (Kenny Roberts) — Parts Catalog

> 1985 Yamaha **RZ350**, US Kenny Roberts edition — 347cc liquid-cooled two-stroke
> twin, YPVS powervalve, Autolube oil injection. Last US production year.
> Hand-authored catalog. Spec/part numbers are community/spec-sheet values ⚠️ —
> **verify against the Yamaha service manual and the actual bike** before ordering.
> Two-stroke: no valves; fueling, crank seals, and the powervalve are what matter.
