# TZ250 Race Manual

> Working manual for a **mid-1980s Yamaha TZ250** over-the-counter production road
> racer (parallel-twin era, before the 1991 V-twin). **Race-only** — no lights, no
> title, premix only. Core specs are **web-verified**; ⚠️ items and all jetting/timing
> need confirming against the **Yamaha TZ tuning manual** for the exact model year.
> Everything here is track/paddock discipline, not street maintenance.

## Identification
- **Model:** Yamaha **TZ250** production racer. Yamaha built TZ250s from **1973–2004**;
  the mid-'80s cars are **parallel twins**. The **90° V-twin** arrives **1991** — a
  different engine, so confirm this bike's exact year/model code before ordering top-end.
- **Heritage:** derived from Yamaha's **OW-series** factory GP machines; the TZ was the
  privateer's 250 GP/club weapon for two decades.
- **Not a TZR.** The street **TZR250** is a detuned, lit, road-legal cousin — do not use
  TZR specs for this bike.

## Premix & oil (verified: no injection)
- **Premix only.** There is no Autolube — you mix race two-stroke oil into the fuel at the
  tuned ratio, **every fill**. A missed mix (straight gas) or the wrong ratio **seizes** it.
- Label the churn/jug; keep the exact ratio in the log. Confirm the ratio for your oil +
  state of tune ⚠️ (race ratios commonly live in the ~24:1–40:1 range depending on oil/tune —
  **use the tuner's number**, not a guess).
- Race fuel spec (octane/leaded) per the engine build ⚠️.

## Fueling & jetting (2× Mikuni flat-slide)
Jetting is **the** tuning task on a race two-stroke and changes with air density:
1. Set mains/pilots/needles/powerjet for the **day's air** (temp, humidity, altitude, baro).
2. Do a **plug chop**: full-throttle run in a high gear, kill + clutch in, coast to a stop,
   read the plug color. Adjust and repeat.
3. **Re-jet whenever anything changes** — chambers, air, altitude, or a fresh top-end.
4. Default safe direction: **jet rich, read the plug** — a lean TZ is a seized TZ, and there
   is no oil injection to save it.

## Top-end (life-limited consumable)
- **Pistons + rings are scheduled wear items**, tracked by **race-hours**, not miles.
  Measure bore + ring end-gap; renew on the build's cadence; carry pre-graded spares in the paddock.
- **Crank** (rods/bearings/seals) is a scheduled rebuild — **pressure-test the crankcase**;
  an air leak leans it out and seizes it.
- **Reeds:** inspect petals each freshen; chipped petals cost drive.

## Chassis & running gear
- **Brakes:** dual 280 mm front, single 210 mm rear. Bed pads; flush race fluid (DOT 4/5.1);
  bleed thoroughly; inspect lines.
- **Gearing:** sprockets set **per circuit**. Log final-drive ratios against trap speed and
  corner-exit rpm; carry a range of teeth.
- **Suspension:** fully adjustable race kit — record clicks (comp/rebound/preload) per session.
- **Tires:** slicks by compound + track temp; use warmers; **log heat cycles**.

## Paddock / pre-session checklist
1. **Mix premix** at the tuned ratio — never straight gas.
2. **Crankcase pressure test + compression** — prove seals + top-end.
3. **Set jetting** for the day's air; do plug chops.
4. **Log race-hours** on the top end; carry spares.
5. Warm coolant; chain slack + sprockets geared for the circuit.
6. Tire warmers; brake bleed; suspension baseline recorded.

## Diagnosis (race two-stroke — no OBD)
No ECU, no codes. Tune by evidence:
- **Plug chops** → jetting.
- **Compression + crankcase pressure** → engine health.
- **Data / trap speed** → gearing.
- Rebuild **log by race-hours**.
Default: **jet rich, read the plug** — and remember there's no oil injection to fall back on.
