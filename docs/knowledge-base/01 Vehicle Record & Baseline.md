# 01 · Vehicle Record & Baseline Inspection

> Establish the car's true health at ~86k before any mods. Full version → [Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/10awNBARgLI7gXAnDpx5c5tDYI73FJVzy9eszLQ06kz4). See also [[VEHICLE]].

**Identity:** 2017 Focus ST1 · VIN 1FADP3L94HL223134 · 2.0 GTDI · Getrag MMT6 · ~86,000 mi · one prior owner, docs to ~60k.

## Verify on the car (unknowns)
Infotainment config (APIM/ACM firmware) · intake brand + MAF housing dia · **Torque Solutions mount** exact part/durometer · ECU tune / Accessport married? · downpipe/exhaust · intercooler brand · suspension parts · wheel/tire size+offset+date codes.

## Baseline procedure (order)
1. **Docs/photos** — VIN, emissions + option labels, odometer; every non-stock splice/fuse/ground.
2. **Full module scan** (OBDLink MX+) — all modules, save current/pending/history/permanent + freeze frames + battery V; don't clear until saved.
3. **Engine mechanical** — cold/hot start, oil (fuel dilution/coolant/debris), coolant, leak hunt (valve cover, turbo lines, oil pan, filter housing); compression/leakdown if history unknown or symptoms.
4. **Ignition/fuel** — pull plugs, record brand/gap/wear/deposits by cylinder; coils.
5. **EVAP/tank** — post-refuel stumble? hard fill? tank deformation? P0456/P144A/P1450 → [[04 Recalls & TSBs]].
6. **Turbo/charge-air** — inspect every joint, witness marks, smoke/pressure test.
7. **Cooling** — pressure test, fans, radiator/condenser blockage, ducting.
8. **MMT6/clutch/shifter** — engagement, slip, cable/bushings, mounts before synchros.
9. **Mounts/driveline**, **brakes/suspension/steering**, **wheels/tires** (torque 100 lb-ft), **body/interior electrical**.

## Approval to start mods
All safety-critical defects fixed · recalls resolved/documented · no active misfire/fuel/overboost/overheat/brake fault · fluid ages known · tune + hardware identified · tires/brakes suitable · all aftermarket wiring mapped + fused.
