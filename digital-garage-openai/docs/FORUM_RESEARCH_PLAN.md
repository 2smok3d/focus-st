# Forum Research Plan

## Purpose
Continuously mine Focus ST communities for real-world observations that formal service literature may not capture, while preserving source authority boundaries.

## Research buckets
1. Diagnostics and repair outcomes: P04DB/PCV, purge/EVAP, underboost/overboost, misfire, fuel-pressure faults, cooling leaks, starting/charging, module/network faults.
2. Powertrain durability: MMT6 synchros, clutch/hydraulics, mounts, axles, wheel hop, turbo/wastegate, charge pipes, bypass valves, HPFP/injector limitations.
3. Cooling and hot-climate behavior: radiator, intercooler, oil cooler, thermostat, coolant, heat soak, Phoenix/Desert Southwest cases.
4. Modification pathways: stock turbo, 250/275/300 WHP, E30/E blends, pump gas, big turbo, intercooler, downpipe, intake, charge pipes, RMM, suspension, brakes, tires.
5. Track use: pads, fluid, tires, alignment, cooling, oil temps, failure reports, post-track inspection.
6. Chassis/interior/electronics: wheel bearings, bushings, end links, shocks, head units, Maestro, SYNC, audio, lighting.

## Extraction schema
For each thread/post capture: source URL, thread title, date, author/role if relevant, model year, mileage, climate, fuel, tune, modifications, symptom, DTCs, tests, repair/action, result, duration after repair, corroboration count, counterexamples, confidence and OEM cross-reference.

## Rules
- Never turn one anecdote into a canonical fault pattern.
- Prefer multi-user convergence and posts that include measured data or confirmed repair outcomes.
- Distinguish correlation from causation.
- Flag vendor-sponsored posts and manufacturer claims.
- Re-check part numbers and procedures against Ford/manufacturer sources.
- Preserve disagreements rather than flattening them into false consensus.
