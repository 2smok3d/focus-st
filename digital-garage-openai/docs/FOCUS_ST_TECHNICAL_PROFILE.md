# 2017 Focus ST — Technical Profile

## Canonical OEM baseline
Ford's ST-specific supplement identifies the North American car as a 2.0L GTDI EcoBoost I4 with a Getrag-Ford MMT6 six-speed manual transaxle. The supplement lists 150-bar high-pressure fuel system, 57-mm throttle body, cast-aluminum pistons, forged-steel connecting rods, cast-iron crankshaft, single-scroll turbo and up to 21 psi boost depending on fuel and atmosphere. It also lists ST-specific suspension rates and bar dimensions.

Ford's current ST online oil specification gives 5.3 L including filter and SAE 5W-30 meeting WSS-M2C961-A1. The spark-plug range published in the ST material is 0.027–0.031 in. The garage keeps these values source-versioned because Ford's online manuals explicitly note that current web content can differ from the printed manual supplied with the vehicle.

## Maintenance baseline
The 2017 Focus normal schedule uses the Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor with an absolute maximum of 10,000 mi/12 months; cabin filter 20,000 mi; engine air filter 30,000 mi; initial coolant 100,000 mi/6 years then 50,000 mi/3 years; spark plugs 100,000 mi; manual-transmission fluid 150,000 mi. Hotter-than-average operation is explicitly listed by Ford as severe driving. The garage stores a second, clearly labeled Phoenix/tuned/track strategy rather than rewriting the Ford baseline.

## Track
Ford Performance's ST supplement says intended track users should perform the multipoint inspection and maintenance associated with the 150,000-mile normal schedule before and after track use, and warns competition use can affect warranty coverage. That unusual factory statement is retained verbatim only by reference; the garage translates it into a detailed pre/post-event checklist.

## Verification rule
Torque specs, internal engine clearances, module pinouts, connector views, wiring procedures, restraint procedures and programming steps are not accepted from forum posts. They require the exact Ford workshop/wiring source or an applicable component-manufacturer procedure.
