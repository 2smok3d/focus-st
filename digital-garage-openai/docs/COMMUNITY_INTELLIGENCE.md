# Focus ST Community & Forum Intelligence

Store each forum claim with URL/thread/post, author/date, model year, mileage, mods, symptom, evidence, action, outcome, corroborations, counterexamples and confidence.

## Recurring themes
- Purge/EVAP: large body of community experience, but Ford recall/service actions take precedence. DIY serviceable assemblies remain experimental.
- Rear motor mount: commonly used to reduce powertrain movement/wheel hop; firmer designs trade improved control for added NVH.
- Intercooler: repeated tuner/owner evidence supports charge-air cooling as foundational for tuned/repeated-pull/hot-climate use. Measure before/after IAT/charge temperature.
- Stock turbo: high-200s to around 300 WHP is achievable in tuner case studies with the right intercooling, calibration and fuel, but results vary heavily.
- Spark plugs: colder plugs/tighter gaps are calibration-specific; store plug/gap requirements per tune revision.
