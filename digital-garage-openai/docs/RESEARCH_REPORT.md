# Digital Garage Research Report — Foundation

The garage separates vehicle observations, Ford/OEM facts, regulatory facts, aftermarket manufacturer claims, community consensus and anecdotes.

## Independent validation
- A legacy repo file says MT82; the Focus ST uses the MMT6 six-speed manual family. Legacy notes are evidence, not truth.
- Ford's ST supplement says track users should perform multi-point inspection and maintenance associated with the 150,000-mile normal schedule before and after track use; competition use can affect warranty.
- A 2026 recall affects certain 2012–2018 Focus vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under the earlier purge-valve recall; VIN applicability is live data.
- Independent tuner evidence repeatedly places the stock turbo as the principal restriction near the high-200s/roughly-300 WHP region, dependent on fuel, intercooling, calibration and conditions. This is not Ford approval.
