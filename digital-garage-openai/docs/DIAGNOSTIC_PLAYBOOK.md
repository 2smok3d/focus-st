# Diagnostic Playbook

1. Define symptom. 2. Record mileage/ambient/fuel/tune/mods/recent work. 3. Preserve pre-repair scan. 4. Record DTC status/module. 5. Preserve freeze frame. 6. Verify battery/charging. 7. Least-invasive tests first. 8. Compare commanded vs actual. 9. Verify power/ground/reference/signal/mechanical basics. 10. Change one variable at a time. 11. Re-test original conditions. 12. Preserve post-repair verification.

## P04DB
Inspect crankcase ventilation routing, connections, restrictions/leaks and modifications; verify tune expectations; use appropriate smoke/pressure methods; record trims/crankcase behavior/oil leakage. Do not hide a physical fault in calibration. Permanent codes can persist until monitor criteria are completed.

## CAN
Read-only captures by default. Use vcan for development. Arbitrary CAN transmission is intentionally not implemented.
