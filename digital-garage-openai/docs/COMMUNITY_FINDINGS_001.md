# Community Findings — Initial Pass

## Rear motor mount
Cross-community discussion and manufacturer feedback converge on the same practical pattern: firmer rear motor mounts reduce powertrain movement and wheel hop but can add idle/low-rpm NVH. Product design and bushing stiffness matter. The garage should track NVH tolerance and mount design rather than universally ranking one mount.

## Purge valve / EVAP
Focus communities document extensive purge-valve troubleshooting and DIY serviceability approaches. Because the same subsystem is also subject to Ford/NHTSA service actions, community fixes should be treated as field evidence only until checked against recall applicability and Ford procedures.

## Stock-turbo performance
Independent tuner case studies repeatedly show the stock turbo operating near its practical upper range around high-200s to roughly 300 WHP with optimized intercooling, calibration and fuel. Dyno method, fuel blend, ambient conditions and engine health create large variation. A reliable daily-driver build should prioritize repeatable temperature and knock control rather than chasing one peak dyno number.

## Intercooler / hot weather
Owner and tuner practice strongly favors an upgraded intercooler before aggressive tuning or repeated pulls. For Phoenix use, the garage should log ambient temperature, charge-air temperature, coolant temperature and repeat-pull recovery so the modification is verified with data.

## Evidence handling
These are community/aftermarket findings, not Ford service specifications. Each detailed claim added later should retain a URL/thread/post/date and be cross-referenced to higher-authority sources where available.
