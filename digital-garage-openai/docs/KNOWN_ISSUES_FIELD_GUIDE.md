# Focus ST Known Issues & Field Guide

This guide is a hypothesis map, not a substitute for diagnosis.

## EVAP / purge valve
High-priority because it has both broad owner history and formal Ford/NHTSA actions. Symptoms reported across service information and owners include drivability complaints, tank vacuum/fuel-gauge anomalies and stalling. For any relevant complaint: record DTCs and freeze frame first, check VIN-specific campaign status, then test rather than shotgun-replacing the assembly.

## P04DB / crankcase ventilation
Treat modifications as part of the system. Intake changes, catch cans, altered PCV plumbing or restrictions can change expected crankcase-flow behavior on EcoBoost applications. The case template explicitly records the complete ventilation topology before parts are replaced. Do not suppress the code as a first-line repair.

## MMT6 shift quality / synchro complaints
Forum history contains repeated second/third gear engagement complaints, but a hard shift is not automatically a synchro. The diagnostic order is clutch release/hydraulics, shifter cables/linkage, mounts and powertrain movement, correct fluid/spec/state, then internal transmission diagnosis. A transmission removal should be supported by evidence.

## Clutch hydraulics
Community reports identify clutch-pedal sticking and internal slave/release issues on Ford ST applications. Because brake and clutch hydraulics share fluid supply architecture, document fluid condition, leaks, bleed response and actual release behavior before transmission removal.

## Cooling / hot climate
Owner reports include aging quick-connect/heater hoses, fan-control failures and radiator/intercooler airflow interactions. Phoenix operation increases the importance of baselining fan operation, coolant pressure/leaks, coolant concentration, charge-temperature recovery and radiator airflow rather than assuming a larger radiator is always the first upgrade.

## Wheel-end noise
Community cases reinforce a basic diagnostic lesson: tire cupping can imitate bearing noise, and improper bearing pressing can contribute to early repeat failure. Store tire condition, sound-vs-load behavior, play/roughness and installer procedure.
